# Research Template Video Content Guide

This guide documents what type of information each research template produces, with examples and recommended content highlights for creating engaging video reports. Focus is on **hidden insights, objective perspectives, and topic analysis** rather than metrics or confidence scores.

---

## 1. Investigative Template

**Purpose:** Deep investigative journalism with actor and relationship analysis

### Information Types Produced

| Finding Type | Description | Video Value |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| `actor` | People, organizations, entities involved with roles/affiliations | **HIGH** - Who are the key players? |
| `event` | Key incidents, actions, decisions with dates and outcomes | **HIGH** - What happened and when? |
| `relationship` | Connections between actors (personal, professional, political) | **HIGH** - Hidden connections exposed |
| `financial` | Money movements, transactions, payments, settlements | **CRITICAL** - Follow the money |
| `evidence` | Documents, statements, data points with verification status | **MEDIUM** - Supporting facts |
| `pattern` | Recurring behaviors, methods, structures | **HIGH** - Systematic behavior revealed |
| `gap` | Missing information, unanswered questions | **MEDIUM** - What we still don't know |

### Expert Perspectives

- **Forensic Financial** - Follow the money, fraud detection
- **Power Network** - Map influence networks and institutional capture
- **Psychological Behavioral** - Analyze motivations and credibility
- **Legal Liability** - Assess legal exposure and enforcement risk
- **Geopolitical Strategic** - Strategic interests and power dynamics

### Video Content Highlights

#### FOCUS ON (Users Will Love):
- **"Follow The Money" sequences** - Visualize financial trails between actors
- **Hidden connections exposed** - Who knows who, and why it matters
- **Timeline reveals** - Events that media missed or downplayed
- **Pattern recognition** - "This isn't the first time X has done Y"
- **Cui bono analysis** - Who benefits from this situation?
- **Contradictions between stated and actual behavior**
- **Power network visualization** - Who has leverage over whom

#### AVOID (Low Value):
- Raw confidence percentages
- Source credibility scores
- Technical finding type labels

### Example Narratives for Video

```
"Three separate shell companies, all registered within the same week,
all sharing the same registered agent, all receiving contracts from
the same agency official who would later join one of them as VP..."

"The official story is market forces. But when you trace the money,
a different picture emerges: $2.3M in consulting fees to a company
owned by the regulator's brother-in-law, paid 3 weeks before the
decision..."
```

## 2. Financial Template

**Purpose:** Stock and financial analysis for investment research

### Information Types Produced

| Finding Type | Description | Video Value |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| `bullish_signal` | Positive earnings, upgrades, growth acceleration | **HIGH** - What's going right |
| `bearish_signal` | Misses, downgrades, declining metrics | **HIGH** - Warning signs |
| `risk` | Business, market, regulatory, competitive risks | **HIGH** - What could go wrong |
| `red_flag` | Accounting irregularities, insider selling, investigations | **CRITICAL** - Danger signals |
| `financial_metric` | Revenue, EPS, margins, price targets | **MEDIUM** - Context only |
| `prediction` | Forward guidance, analyst forecasts | **HIGH** - Future outlook |

### Expert Perspectives

- **Institutional Investor** - Long-term value creation, moats, management
- **Short Seller** - Red flags, fraud detection, skeptical analysis
- **Quantitative Risk** - Tail risks, stress testing, correlations
- **Activist Investor** - Value creation levers, governance, catalysts
- **Macro Strategist** - Economic cycles, policy risks, global context

### Video Content Highlights

#### FOCUS ON (Users Will Love):
- **Bull vs Bear narrative tension** - Present both sides fairly
- **What Wall Street isn't saying** - Hidden risks, overlooked factors
- **Ecosystem dependencies** - Top customers, supplier concentration
- **Kill criteria** - What would invalidate the investment thesis?
- **Contrarian perspectives** - Why skeptics might be right
- **Money flows** - Who's buying, who's selling, and why?
- **Historical parallels** - Similar situations and how they played out

#### AVOID (Low Value):
- Raw P/E ratios without context
- Analyst rating counts
- Basic revenue numbers without analysis

### Example Narratives for Video

```

"The short sellers have been wrong for 3 years. But they're making

that nobody seems to have noticed..."
```

## Universal Video Content Principles

### What Users Want to See

1. **Hidden insights** - Information not obvious from surface research
2. **Objective perspectives** - Multiple viewpoints, fair analysis
3. **Topic analysis** - Deep understanding, not surface summaries
4. **Pattern recognition** - Connections others miss
5. **Follow the money** - Financial motivations revealed
6. **Counter-narratives** - Alternative explanations considered
7. **Real voices** - Actual user experiences, not marketing
8. **Action implications** - What this means for decisions

### What Users Don't Want

1. **Confidence scores** - Don't show "87% confidence"
2. **LLM metrics** - No token counts, processing stats
3. **Raw data tables** - Data without narrative
4. **Generic summaries** - Surface-level overviews
5. **Marketing language** - Promotional framing
6. **Exhaustive lists** - Everything without prioritization

### Video Scene Recommendations by Template

| Template | Opening Hook | Core Narrative | Closing Impact |
|----------|-------------|----------------|----------------|
| Investigative | "What they don't want you to know..." | Follow the money trail | Implications for accountability |
| Financial | "Wall Street says X, but..." | Bull vs Bear tension | Kill criteria / decision point |
| Competitive | "The battle you're not seeing..." | Strategic dynamics | Winner/loser prediction |
| Legal | "This ruling changes everything..." | Precedent implications | Who's at risk now |
| Tech Market | "The hype says X, developers say..." | Reality vs expectations | Where to bet next |
| Contract | "Your tax dollars at work..." | Corruption pattern exposed | What should happen |
| Understanding | "The story you were told vs..." | Full context revealed | Why it matters |
| Due Diligence | "Before you sign anything..." | Red flag compilation | The verdict |
| Purchase | "Before you click buy..." | Hidden truth revealed | Better alternatives |
| Reputation | "Is this a scam? We investigated..." | Warning signs exposed | The verdict |
