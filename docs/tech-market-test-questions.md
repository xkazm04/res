# Tech Market Analysis - Annual Test Questions

These 10 questions represent topics that companies, analysts, and technology leaders revisit annually. Use these to test the Tech Market module's output quality against real industry reports.

## Reference Reports for Validation

- Gartner Hype Cycle for Emerging Technologies
- Forrester Tech Trends Report
- ThoughtWorks Technology Radar
- Stack Overflow Developer Survey
- State of DevOps Report (DORA/Google Cloud)
- GitHub Octoverse Report
- JetBrains State of Developer Ecosystem
- O'Reilly Technology Trends Report
- McKinsey Technology Trends Outlook
- Sequoia/a16z Annual Market Reports

## Test Questions

### 1. AI Coding Assistants Adoption & Impact
**Question:** What is the current adoption rate of AI coding assistants (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude, etc.) among professional developers, and what measurable productivity impact are enterprises reporting?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Adoption percentage among developers
- Productivity gains (lines of code, time savings, PR velocity)
- Enterprise vs. individual adoption rates
- Top tools by market share

**Comparable reports:** GitHub Octoverse, Stack Overflow Survey, JetBrains Survey

---

### 2. Cloud Infrastructure Cost Optimization
**Question:** What are the top cloud cost optimization strategies enterprises are adopting, and what is the current state of FinOps maturity across organizations?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Average cloud waste percentage
- FinOps adoption rates
- Top cost optimization techniques (spot instances, rightsizing, reserved capacity)
- Multi-cloud vs. single-cloud cost implications

**Comparable reports:** Flexera State of the Cloud, HashiCorp State of Cloud Strategy

---

### 3. Platform Engineering & Internal Developer Platforms
**Question:** How widespread is platform engineering adoption, and what are the key components of successful Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs)?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Platform engineering team prevalence
- Common IDP components (Backstage, Port, etc.)
- Developer self-service capabilities
- Time-to-production improvements

**Comparable reports:** Gartner Platform Engineering, Puppet State of DevOps, CNCF Survey

---

### 4. Kubernetes & Container Orchestration Maturity
**Question:** What percentage of organizations are running Kubernetes in production, and what are the primary operational challenges they face?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Kubernetes production adoption rate
- Container runtime preferences
- Top operational challenges (security, networking, observability)
- Managed vs. self-hosted Kubernetes ratio

**Comparable reports:** CNCF Annual Survey, Datadog Container Report

---

### 5. Developer Experience & Productivity Metrics
**Question:** What are the leading indicators of developer productivity that organizations are measuring, and how do top-performing engineering teams differ from average ones?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- DORA metrics adoption (deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate)
- Developer satisfaction/experience scores
- Cognitive load and context switching impact
- Elite vs. low performer differentiators

**Comparable reports:** DORA State of DevOps, DX Developer Experience Survey, LinearB benchmarks

---

### 6. Enterprise AI/ML Infrastructure Investment
**Question:** What are enterprises investing in for AI/ML infrastructure, and how is the build vs. buy decision evolving for foundation models?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- GPU/compute infrastructure spending trends
- Foundation model API vs. self-hosted model preferences
- MLOps platform adoption (MLflow, Kubeflow, etc.)
- AI infrastructure budget as percentage of IT spend

**Comparable reports:** a16z AI Infrastructure Report, O'Reilly AI Adoption Survey

---

### 7. Security Posture & Shift-Left Adoption
**Question:** How effectively are organizations implementing shift-left security practices, and what is the current state of software supply chain security?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- SAST/DAST/SCA tool adoption rates
- SBOM (Software Bill of Materials) implementation
- Mean time to remediate vulnerabilities
- Security training frequency for developers

**Comparable reports:** Snyk State of Open Source Security, Synopsys OSSRA Report, Sonatype State of Software Supply Chain

---

### 8. Low-Code/No-Code Platform Adoption
**Question:** What is the enterprise adoption rate of low-code/no-code platforms, and which use cases are seeing the highest success rates?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Low-code platform market penetration
- Citizen developer programs prevalence
- Common use case categories (internal tools, workflows, customer-facing apps)
- Shadow IT concerns and governance approaches

**Comparable reports:** Gartner Low-Code Magic Quadrant, Forrester Low-Code Wave

---

### 9. Observability & AIOps Maturity
**Question:** How are organizations evolving their observability practices, and what role is AI playing in incident detection and response?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Three pillars (logs, metrics, traces) consolidation trends
- OpenTelemetry adoption rates
- AIOps/ML-powered alerting usage
- MTTR improvements with advanced observability

**Comparable reports:** Datadog State of Observability, Splunk State of Observability, New Relic Observability Forecast

---

### 10. Remote/Hybrid Engineering Team Effectiveness
**Question:** How are distributed engineering teams performing compared to co-located teams, and what tools/practices correlate with high-performing remote teams?

**Key metrics to validate:**
- Remote vs. hybrid vs. in-office developer distribution
- Async communication tool adoption
- Documentation-first culture indicators
- Collaboration overhead metrics

**Comparable reports:** GitHub Octoverse, Buffer State of Remote Work, Atlassian Teamwork Survey

## Evaluation Criteria

When comparing Tech Market module outputs against real reports, assess:

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Accuracy** | Do the numbers/percentages align with industry reports (within 10-15% margin)? |
| **Recency** | Is the data current (2024-2025) rather than outdated? |
| **Nuance** | Does it capture industry segmentation (enterprise vs. SMB, by region)? |
| **Trends** | Are directional trends (up/down/stable) correctly identified? |
| **Sources** | Are findings backed by credible sources? |
| **Predictions** | Are 2026 predictions reasonable extrapolations with confidence ranges? |

## Suggested Test Workflow

1. Run each question through the Tech Market template
2. Collect the generated findings, trends, and predictions
3. Cross-reference with 2-3 real industry reports
4. Score on the criteria above (1-5 scale)
5. Document gaps and areas for improvement
