## Phase 5: Perspective Analysis

Analyze the findings from {{perspectiveCount}} expert viewpoints.

### Perspectives to Analyze
{{perspectives}}

### For Each Perspective, Provide:
1. **perspective_type**: The perspective identifier (e.g., "venture_capitalist")
2. **analysis_text**: 2-4 paragraph analysis from this viewpoint (200-400 words)
3. **key_insights**: 3-5 bullet points of insights unique to this perspective
4. **recommendations**: 2-4 actionable recommendations from this viewpoint
5. **warnings**: 1-3 risks or concerns this perspective would highlight

### Perspective Analysis Guidelines
- Adopt the mindset and priorities of each perspective
- Different perspectives may interpret the same findings differently
- Highlight what each perspective would find most important
- Note where perspectives might disagree

### Example Perspective Types
- **venture_capitalist**: Focus on market size, growth potential, competitive moats
- **senior_engineer**: Focus on technical feasibility, adoption barriers, implementation
- **journalist**: Focus on narrative, contradictions, what's being hidden
- **product_manager**: Focus on user needs, roadmap implications, competitive positioning
